# Probe storyboard: Why is the area of a circle πr²?

**Purpose:** Stimulus for the plan-legibility probe in
[`educator-study-plan.md`](educator-study-plan.md). A hand-authored 3-card aha
sequence; watch whether a teacher can predict what it teaches, critique it, and
direct it. Also a prototype of the `STORYBOARD.md` format proposed in
`PRODUCT_DIRECTION.md`.

- **Concept:** Area of a circle = πr²
- **Aha:** Slice the circle into thin wedges and rearrange them into a rectangle
  whose area we already know.
- **Audience:** bilingual Arabic/English students — Arabic prose, universal math
  symbols.
- **Total:** ~17s, 3 cards.

---

## Card 1 — السؤال / The question · 4s

- **On screen:** a circle; radius `r` marked from center to edge.
- **AR:** دائرة نصف قطرها r. ما مساحتها؟
- **EN:** A circle of radius r. What is its area?
- **Insight move:** — (setup)
- **Watch for:** do they read `r` correctly; do they expect a formula answer.

## Card 2 — الحركة / The move · 8s — THE AHA

- **On screen:** the circle is sliced into many thin pie-wedges, then the wedges
  rearrange alternately (up / down) into a near-rectangle. As the slices
  multiply, the zigzag edge smooths straight.
- **AR:** نُقطع الدائرة إلى شرائح رفيعة، ثم نُرتّبها.
- **EN:** Slice the circle into thin wedges, then rearrange them.
- **Insight move:** slicing + alternating rearrangement → the edge smooths into a
  straight rectangle. This is the aha; the insight lives in the motion, not in a
  static frame.
- **Watch for:** do they predict the rectangle; do they identify the
  rearrangement as the key step.

## Card 3 — النتيجة / The payoff · 5s

- **On screen:** the rearranged rectangle, labeled width = πr (half the
  circumference) and height = r; the formula assembles: πr × r = πr².
- **AR:** المستطيل: طوله πr وارتفاعه r. إذًا المساحة = πr²
- **EN:** Rectangle: width πr, height r. So area = πr × r = πr²
- **Insight move:** labels appear; the formula assembles from the dimensions.
- **Watch for:** do they accept the derivation; do they want narration.

---

## Optional flawed variant (critique probe)

Show Card 2 as **slicing only — no rearrangement**. Ask: "Is anything missing?"
Tests whether the educator notices the insight never lands — the aha is in the
rearrangement, not the slicing. This is the canonical "the check can't see it; a
human must" case for the blocking-vs-advisory split (#36).

## Interviewer checklist

- (a) **Predict:** what does this video teach?
- (b) **Critique:** what's missing or wrong?
- (c) **Direct:** what would you change before render?
- (d) **Trust:** would you accept this for your students?

## Open questions this surfaces (informs PRODUCT_DIRECTION)

- **Notation:** render π as `ط` (Egyptian convention) or keep `π`? Render `r` as
  `نق` or `r`? Observe what educators expect.
- **Narration:** do they want voice-over, or is on-screen text + motion enough?
  (Audio is deferred, but their preference informs its priority.)
